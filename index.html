About me

I look for what actually limits a model, then resolve that limit through analysis and experiment. My work runs along two directions: efficient LLMs through architecture compression, and grounding vision-language-action models in speech.

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Ajou University, and a member of the MMAI Lab (Multi-Modal Artificial Intelligence).
Advised by Prof. Jongbin Ryu.

In progress

  • Speech-conditioned VLA policies

    Vision-language-action models cannot resolve ambiguous deixis — spatial referring expressions such as “that one over there.” Speech-spatial conditioning grounds the referred object from the speaker’s location, so the policy acts on what was actually meant.
